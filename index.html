How we work

A clear process from idea to delivery.

Good projects need more than technical skill. They need clear expectations, communication and careful review.

01

Understand

We learn about the goal, users, requirements and constraints.

02

Plan

We define the scope, approach, deliverables and practical next steps.

03

Build

We design, develop and implement the agreed solution.

04

Review

We test, refine and incorporate useful feedback before delivery.

05

Deliver & support

We deliver the finished work and can provide continued support where applicable.

Our approach

Professional work starts with trust.

Clear scope

We aim to make deliverables and expectations understandable before work begins.

Clear communication

Clients should know what is happening and have a clear way to ask questions.

Practical technology

We choose tools and approaches based on the problem, not simply because they are fashionable.

Long-term thinking

Where appropriate, we consider maintenance, updates, usability and future growth from the start.

Bluebird grew from a simple idea: learn technology, build useful things, and make those skills available to other people and organizations.
