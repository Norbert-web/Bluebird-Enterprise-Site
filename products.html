Flagship — Android · Kotlin · Jetpack Compose

Bluebird OS

A free, open-source Android OS simulator and launcher that makes your phone feel like a real desktop computer — without rooting. It runs a full Android environment inside your device: windowed apps, a taskbar, a built-in app suite, and a desktop mode that works on any phone. Currently on v1.7 stable, with v1.8 in active development.

  • Desktop Mode — windowed experience with taskbar and multitasking, works with external monitors or in-hand
  • OS Simulator + Launcher — use as your home launcher or a full environment simulator, no root required
  • Lightweight — engineered for budget and mid-range Android devices, API 26+ (Android 8.0+)
  • 100% free & open source — MIT license, no ads, no telemetry, no tracking
