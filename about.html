About

Developers first. A company because of it.

Bluebird Enterprise is proudly built by the LAMN-NOBERT team in Uganda — part of the growing East African tech movement, proving that world-class, impactful software and services can be built right here on the continent.

Origin

It started with curiosity.

Before there was a company, there was a project: Bluebird OS, a free and open-source Android launcher that gives any phone a real desktop mode. No VC funding, no investors — just developers who loved Android and believed in open source.

That project needed a website, then needed hosting, then needed design work, and the people building it turned out to be good at all of it. Bluebird Enterprise grew out of that: the same team, doing for other people — schools, churches, small businesses — what they'd already been doing for their own software.

Today

A multidisciplinary technology company

Bluebird Enterprise now covers four departments — digital presence, creative & design, physical & print content, and hardware & technical services — while still building and maintaining Bluebird OS and a growing list of independent software projects.

We're built for East Africa and the global majority first: lightweight software that runs on affordable hardware, and service pricing that matches the local market.

Who we are

Self-taught, and building anyway

Most of our team is self-taught. Several of us are still students, some close to graduating. We build with the help of modern tools, including AI, and we've found that consistent effort closes the gap a certificate would otherwise mark — what you can actually do carries the work.

That's also why the Schools Program exists: most schools in Uganda still don't have a website, and we'd rather make that achievable at a fair price than leave it out of reach. Not every school we approach will say yes — but we keep reaching out anyway, because the ones who do say yes are worth it.

How we work

The standard job, start to finish

Every job — a website, a flyer, a broken phone — moves through the same four steps.

01

Inquiry comes in

Client Relations receives the request by WhatsApp, phone, or email.

02

Routed to a head

Website → Technical. Flyer/photo → Creative. Broken phone → Operations & Hardware.

03

Quote delivered

The department head estimates cost and timeline; Client Relations delivers the quote.

04

Work begins

Once payment is confirmed by Finance & Admin, the job starts.

Accountability

How we stay on schedule

Every Monday morning, each department head sends the CEO a brief update on current project status. Every Friday afternoon, the whole board meets to review the week's work, handle client feedback, announce new jobs, and plan the week ahead.

Once a month, on the last Friday, the team runs a Skill Share — department heads teach each other, from web security to advanced photo editing — so the company's capability grows without needing to hire.
