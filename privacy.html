Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Bluebird Enterprise ("we", "us", "our") operates bluebirduganda.dpdns.org, including the Bluebird OS product site. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or contact us, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide directly — when you submit our contact form or email/call/WhatsApp us, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if given), and the content of your message.
  • Information collected automatically — our analytics tool (Section 3) records aggregated data such as pages visited, referring site, device/browser type, and approximate location derived from IP address. We do not use this to personally identify you.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver the service or project you've requested
  • To understand how visitors use our site, so we can improve it
  • To meet legal or security obligations, such as investigating a reported vulnerability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Analytics

This site uses a third-party analytics script (analytics.digitalplat.org) to understand aggregate traffic patterns. It does not collect names, emails, or form content. You can block it using your browser's tracking-protection settings without affecting your ability to use the site.

4. Cookies

We use only minimal cookies/local storage needed for basic site function (like remembering your light/dark theme choice) and for the analytics tool above. We don't use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

5. Contact form

Messages submitted through our contact form are delivered by email via a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit). The information you submit is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

6. Data retention

We retain inquiry and project-related correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the service and meet legal or tax obligations under Ugandan law, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing trebronwayne@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

8. Third-party links

Our site links to third-party platforms (GitHub, Telegram, Discord, YouTube, X, TikTok, Reddit). Each has its own privacy practices, which we don't control — please review theirs separately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this page.

10. Contact us

Questions can be sent to trebronwayne@gmail.com or nlamn.dev@outlook.com, or by phone/WhatsApp at +256 790 014 428.
