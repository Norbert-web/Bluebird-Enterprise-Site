Reporting structure

Weekly rhythm

Every department head sends the CEO a short update every Monday morning. The full board meets every Friday afternoon (3:00–4:00 PM) to review the week's sales, handle client feedback, announce new jobs, and plan the week ahead.

Growth

Monthly Skill Share

On the last Friday of each month, department heads teach each other — the CTO covers web security basics, the Head of Creative covers advanced photo-editing tools, and so on. It grows the team's capability without hiring.
