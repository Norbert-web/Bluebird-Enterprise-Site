The idea

A digital learning environment built around the local curriculum.

Remote Schools is being designed to organize learning into a clear digital structure rather than simply collecting disconnected resources. The platform is intended to grow into a useful environment for students, educators and education-focused organizations.

01

Curriculum-aligned

Learning content is planned around the Ugandan curriculum so the platform speaks to the education context it is built for.

02

Structured learning

Subjects, topics, lessons, exercises and assessments can be organized into a consistent learning path.

03

Open by design

The project is intended to make learning resources easier to access and organize across different learning situations.

Why it matters

Technology should fit the people it serves.

The project is being developed with a local context in mind: curriculum relevance, practical access to learning and a structure that can grow with the needs of Ugandan learners.

For learners

A clearer place to discover and work through learning materials.

For educators

A structured foundation for organizing curriculum-linked digital learning.

For institutions

A potential digital learning layer that can complement existing education programs.
